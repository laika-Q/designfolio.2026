Overview

Wide Awake PH offers specialty coffee sourced from quality driven products, roasted fresh, light, and with precision in the Philippines. The brand is dedicated to showcasing the potential of Southeast Asian coffee and providing thoughtfully sourced, high-quality beans.

The Brief

For this project, the client wanted a modern-contemporary Bauhaus feel for their e-commerce site, taking inspiration from Petra Roasting Co. and Common Man Roasters.
