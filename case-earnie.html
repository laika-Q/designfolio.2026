Overview

EARNie paves an easier way for freelancers to transact globally as digital first solopreneurs by providing instant, affordable and credible financial and business services for their personal and professional needs.

The Brief

EARNie targets freelancers who treat freelancing as a full time profession and not just a side gig, but are stuck with using informal, non-conventional money management. EARNie closes that gap — giving them trust/legitimacy, reliable payments, low-friction business management, and smarter control over their earnings.

The brand taps into the culture of digital nomads, independent workers that value their time, freedom, and financial wellbeing.
